Trace facts: the times are named constants in `story-data/constants.js` (`TRACE`: start 480 s, 300 s after the trust choice, 120 s after the unlock, 60 s at 8x on the watcher takeover, `hint` costs 30 s). The evidence loss thresholds (`CASE.compromiseAt`) are derived from a share of the window after the trust choice (`CASE.compromiseShare`: 3/8, 7/24, 3/4, which were 45, 35 and 90 s at 120 s and are 113, 88 and 225 s at 300 s); the map (`CASE.traceWindow`) follows the same window. The trace sounds keep their absolute thresholds (warning at 120 s left, critical at 60 s and 10 s): with 300 s the first three minutes are calm. Policy: `running` from the start until the trust choice (floors at 10 s, TRACE PENDING), `lethal` from the choice on, `close()` at the ghost script's first step (TRACE CLOSED).

## Narrative engine (`js/engine.js`; drives the boot, the intro, the trust choice, the routes, the ghost route, the reveal and the endings; the Level 01 shell is the registry-based `level01.js`)

A story is `{ initialState, routes?, segments: [{ id, skippable, announce, nodes }] }`. The engine compiles the segments into one flat program (a `branch` becomes jumps), walks it with a pointer, and asks a **host** (built by `story.js`) to do everything visible. It never touches the DOM, so `node tools/validate-story.mjs` can validate and dry-run a story.

- **Node types** (fields in `NODE_SPECS`, unknown fields are validation errors): output `line blank type say typing cmd bar klog svc memtest`, timing and sound `wait sfx scene`, effects `gfx flash static corrupt blackout rain class logo wipe` (`class` toggles a class on the screen element; `line` accepts `ref` so a later `run` node can reach the element), `trace` (`do`: `start dock boost slow set mode close`), `state`, `case` (a call on the case file), flow `label goto branch choice end`, and `run { fn(host, flow) }` for a bespoke beat. Any field may be a function `(state, host) => value`.
- **Segments and skip.** `skippable: 'boot' | 'intro'` lets the skip control start a skip while the pointer is in the segment. A skip calls `session.flush()` and sets the pace to fast: waits collapse, `sfx`/`gfx`/`flash`/`static`/`corrupt`/`wait` nodes are dropped, text still appears instantly and state changes (`scene`, `trace`, `state`, `case`, `run`) still happen. Entering the next segment ends the fast-forward. `announce: false` keeps screen readers quiet (the BIOS).
- **State** is a small object; `engine.getState()` returns a read-only copy plus the pointer (for the phase 7 inspector). `run({ from: label, state })` starts anywhere (developer routes).
- **Validation** (`tools/validate-story.mjs`): node types and fields, label and `choice.next` targets, audio cues and scenes, glitch names, case file calls, developer-route labels, plus a dry run for every combination of choices.
